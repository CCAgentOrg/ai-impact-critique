How to Build a Counter-Narrative

If you want to replicate this project for other regions or themes:

  1. Select a technology showcase or "AI for good" catalog to red-team.
  2. Apply a harm framework (surveillance, extraction, bias, exclusion) to identify concerning deployments.
  3. Gather sources from academic literature, civil society reports, and community media.
  4. Center the voices of those most impacted, not just Western experts.
  5. Highlight resistance networks and amplify their work.
  6. Develop concrete demands for policymakers and developers.
  7. Publish in accessible formats (web, print, social media) and in local languages.
