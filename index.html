Exposing the Hidden Harms of "AI for Impact"

The AI Impact Commons presents itself as a global catalog of AI solutions for social good. But many of its featured deployments raise serious concerns about surveillance, data extraction, and the export of risk to the Global South. This counter-narrative website examines 10 concerning use cases, providing critical analysis, community voices, and networks of resistance.

Why This Counter-Narrative Matters

The AI Impact Commons functions as an uncritical showcase, dressing potentially harmful systems in "impact" branding. As our analysis reveals, these deployments often:

  • Extract massive amounts of sensitive data from vulnerable populations without meaningful consent
  • Create digital infrastructure that can be repurposed for surveillance and control
  • Prioritize corporate profit and government efficiency over community sovereignty
  • Export risks to the Global South while benefits accrue in the Global North
  • Ignore questions of data ownership, opt-out mechanisms, and downstream misuse

This site aims to rebalance the discourse by centering the perspectives of those most at risk.
