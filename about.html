About This Project

What Is This?

This website is a counter-narrative to the AI Impact Commons, a global catalog that presents AI deployments for social good without critical scrutiny. We examine 10 concerning use cases from the Commons, exposing potential harms, amplifying critical voices, and highlighting resistance networks.

Why We Built This

The AI Impact Commons functions as an uncritical showcase, potentially accelerating the diffusion of harmful AI systems by dressing surveillance and data extraction projects in "impact" branding. Many featured deployments:

  • Extract massive amounts of sensitive data from vulnerable populations with limited consent.
  • Create digital infrastructure that can be repurposed for surveillance and control.
  • Prioritize corporate profit and government efficiency over community sovereignty.
  • Export risks to the Global South while benefits accrue in the Global North.
  • Ignore questions of data ownership, opt-out mechanisms, and downstream misuse.

We believe AI for social good must be grounded in human rights, data sovereignty, and community consent—not just capability and scale.

Who We Are

This project was developed by concerned researchers and digital rights advocates who believe in technology that serves people, not power. We are not affiliated with the AI Impact Commons, its co-chairs, or any of the listed deploying organizations.

Our team conducted a red-team analysis of the AI Impact Commons website in February 2026, identifying the top 10 concerning use cases and gathering critical perspectives from academic research, NGO reports, and community voices.

Our Approach

We follow a data justice framework, centering the rights of those most impacted by AI systems. Our analysis:

  • Prioritizes Global South perspectives over Western saviorism.
  • Cites sources properly so readers can verify claims.
  • Attributes community quotes with clear provenance.
  • Highlights resistance networks to connect readers with on-the-ground movements.
  • Emphasizes data sovereignty, consent, and community control as non-negotiable principles.

How to Use This Site

Start with the homepage to get an overview, then explore each of the 10 use case pages for detailed analysis. Each page includes:

  • Description from AI Impact Commons
  • Expanded harms and systemic risks
  • Research citations (academic papers, NGO reports)
  • Community voices and activist quotes
  • Networks of resistance in affected regions
  • Key demands for remedying the harms

Use this site as a research tool, advocacy resource, or educational material to spark critical conversations about AI for impact.

Get Involved

This is a living project. If you have additional sources, corrections, or want to contribute to future editions, please reach out. We especially welcome contributions from communities directly affected by these deployments.

Important Disclaimer

This site is for critical education and advocacy. It does not represent the views of the organizations whose work we cite, nor is it affiliated with the AI Impact Commons. We have made efforts to verify all sources but recommend independent verification for research purposes.
